Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up as a Donor? +

You can click the "Join Us" button in the top right corner and select "Sign up as Donor" to register your restaurant or kitchen.

Is the food collection free? +

Yes! We do not charge donors for food collection. Our mission is to reduce waste and feed the needy.

How do you verify NGOs? +

We have a strict verification process involving document checks and physical site visits to ensure safety and transparency.
